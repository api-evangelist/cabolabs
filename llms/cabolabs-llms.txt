# CaboLabs

> CaboLabs is a health informatics company specializing in standards and interoperability. We help healthcare organizations and technology vendors build, assess, buy, improve, audit, and integrate Health Information Systems using international standards: openEHR, HL7 FHIR, HL7 v2.x, CDA, and DICOM.

Founded in 2012. Based in Uruguay, serving clients worldwide. Languages: English and Spanish.
